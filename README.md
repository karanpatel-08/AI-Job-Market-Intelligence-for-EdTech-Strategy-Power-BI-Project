# 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing AI job market trends to understand hiring demand, salary distribution, remote work patterns, and skill requirements using Power BI.

The objective was to transform raw AI job market data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard capable of helping EdTech organizations design smarter, market-aligned educational programs.

The dashboard was developed from a workforce analytics and EdTech strategy perspective to support data-driven curriculum planning and talent market analysis.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The AI industry is evolving rapidly, creating continuous changes in:
- Skill demand
- Hiring patterns
- Salary expectations
- Remote work opportunities
- Global talent requirements

EdTech companies often struggle to align their programs with real-time market demand due to limited visibility into evolving AI hiring trends.

Without data-driven insights, educational platforms may:
- Offer outdated curriculum structures
- Miss emerging skill requirements
- Misalign course offerings with industry demand
- Reduce student employability outcomes

This project addresses these challenges by analyzing AI job market behavior and workforce trends through interactive Power BI reporting.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

## AI Hiring Demand Analysis

The dashboard highlights growing demand across AI-related job roles and identifies major hiring patterns within the AI workforce ecosystem.

This helps stakeholders understand which areas of AI talent demand are expanding most rapidly.

---

## Salary Distribution Insights

The analysis evaluates compensation patterns across:
- Experience levels
- Employment types
- Geographic locations
- Job categories

This helps identify high-value skill segments within the AI industry.

---

## Remote Work Trends

The project analyzes remote work adoption within AI roles and evaluates how distributed work models influence hiring behavior.

This insight helps EdTech platforms understand evolving workplace expectations.

---

## Experience-Level Analysis

The dashboard compares salary and hiring patterns across:
- Entry-level roles
- Mid-level professionals
- Senior AI positions

This supports better career pathway planning and curriculum structuring.

---

## Geographic Workforce Distribution

Regional analysis identifies:
- Major AI hiring locations
- Talent concentration regions
- Global workforce demand patterns

These insights help organizations understand international AI labor market trends.
